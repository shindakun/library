Ebook DRM (Adobe)

Authorize an Adobe account to fulfill and decrypt DRMed books. Use a fresh or throwaway AdobeID. This runs once; the section disappears after.

Sent over your LAN to the local sidecar; credentials are used only to register with Adobe and are not stored (only the resulting activation + key are kept). On a headless host you can instead run the CLI setup; see docs/DEPLOY.md.

Audiobook DRM (Audible)

Store your Audible activation bytes so .aax audiobooks can be decrypted. This runs once; the section disappears after.

Bytes are an account secret, sent over your LAN to the local sidecar and stored only there. Pasting is the reliable path; login retrieval may be unavailable in this build (it then reports a clear error). See docs/DEPLOY.md.
